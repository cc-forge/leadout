## How to submit

1. Read the list's CONTRIBUTING guide and its quality bar (some lists want a minimum age
   or number of stars). Skip lists the project does not meet yet.
2. Add exactly one line, in the right section, in the list's exact format.
3. One pull request per list.
4. Do not follow up or ping the maintainers after submitting.
